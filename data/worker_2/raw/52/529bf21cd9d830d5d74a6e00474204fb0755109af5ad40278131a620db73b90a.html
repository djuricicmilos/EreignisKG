28. Reality

Imagine it like this—as.... This is the relationship of correspondence between knowledge and its object. This correspondence has, from time immemorial, been the hallmark of the essence of truth. Thus, quantum mechanics is linked to the question of the essence of truth and the appropriation of the true. The “fundamental” aspect of this undertaking. Can “science” now determine anything about this? No. But if so, then only insofar as “it” is interpreted “philosophically.” It cannot accomplish this on its own. The first question: Where does the objectivity of physics as such belong?

Where these are impossible, there is no objectivity either. A change in the concept of the object? An object is only an object for a subject; there is no “object in itself.” Whenever we speak of objects, an object is always an object-for-us.

a) That it is for us, and how it is for us,

} that is the question.

b) Where we stand in this regard,

the objectivity of the object and its relation back to representation. The respective world region of the currently guiding representation.

The World and World Domains

World Domain and Field of Objects

The intermingling, confusion, and overlap of the fields of objects within a world that is itself in flux.

“Reality”—what its essence consists of. The attribution of reality to an “object.”
