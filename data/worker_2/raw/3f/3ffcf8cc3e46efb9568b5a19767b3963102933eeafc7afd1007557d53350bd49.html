Geschichte der Philosophie von Thomas von Aquin bis Kant

History of philosophy from Thomas Aquinas to Kant

In his Marburg lecture course from the Winter Semester of 1926/27, Heidegger reconstructs the tradition from the standpoint of phenomenological ontology: how Western thinkers understood Being (Sein), beings, truth, nature, humanity, and God before Kant. Martin Heidegger does not mainly ask, “What doctrines did these philosophers teach?” His deeper question is: what understanding of Being made their philosophies possible? Overall Thesis


The lecture traces the transformation of metaphysics:

Medieval ontology (Aquinas)
→ modern subjectivity (Descartes)
→ absolute substance (Spinoza)
→ monadological metaphysics (Leibniz)
→ rational system metaphysics (Wolff/Crusius)
→ Kant’s transformation of the question of Being

The hidden theme is Heidegger’s own project:

Philosophy = phenomenological ontology = the critical investigation of the meaning of Being.
