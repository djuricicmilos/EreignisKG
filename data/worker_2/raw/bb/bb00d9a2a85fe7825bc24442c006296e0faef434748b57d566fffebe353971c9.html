The “In-the-Middle” only reaches its foundation from Being; insofar as it has thus sprung forth from Being as the “In-the-Middle,” the event becomes the [origin] for the gods—their flight and approach. The rapture into time-space as an entry into what has thus been opened, and the entry as an overtaking (leap-ahead).



11. The Unfolding of Key Questions and the “Inside” (In-the-Middle) and “Outside” (All Around)


1. Being “from within”? Where within, who is “in” Being? We ourselves a) in that we belong to it, b) but this belonging — the midst — we ourselves know (that is, our relation of being to Being as a whole), c) we [?] ourselves are simply — knowing — or rather, not without knowledge (knowledge and will). The “from within” thus points to the undeveloped center and is itself posited only very roughly and without knowledge of the structure (as it were, and as a matter of course). (The strange insensitivity to the gravity of such approaches!!).

2. Being “from the outside”—it as a whole. Who stands outside and how; and around what is the whole concentrated; and how is the surrounding itself posited and in what relation to the center? Crouching down before Being.
