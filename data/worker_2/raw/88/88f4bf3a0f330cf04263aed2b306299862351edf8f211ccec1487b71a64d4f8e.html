16   |   I. Notes to Being and Time

How is it with the approach to the Being-question in Summer Semester 1935 (Introduction to Metaphysics: Why are there beings at all! and not rather nothing?)

The question about the “meaning” (compare to § 32 below!) of beyng is not yet original enough (see below p. 28); but according to Hartmann it is not at all possible to question in such an original way. “Meaning” is what makes intelligibility possible. What the meaning of Being means is not just said in a fundamental way (p. 151 f.), but rather shown in the meaning of the Being of care (the meaning of the Being of Dasein)—§ 61 ff. The question about meaning refers to the understanding-of-Being. This approach is also not thereby overcome when understanding is grasped as projection—what we assuredly find here is the finger pointing at Da-sein.

| The question about the meaning of Being—“meaning” is that to which the understanding opens itself as projection—the open realm—the truth—into which something—here, Being—is placed.

Understanding—but which here, as thrown projection, is actually the basic happening of Da-sein itself—has nothing to do with “thinking” as predicative determination—the Logos of logic. With the understanding-of-Being, Being is thus not related to thinking. Nevertheless, thinking—in the sense of the thoughtful thinking of Being—[19] is conversely to be grasped from just this understanding—but this thinking is itself grounded in the leap-into Da-sein; (event) is not creatively thought, but in the leap-in it is brought into play. --

| The “meaning” of Being is asked about in this way because it is the basic question about Being itself. But Being is no longer in being in the way that beings are—for it would then be based on itself and in accord with itself—but Being itself, questioned only on the basis of its truth, i.e., on the basis of the truth of its essential occurrence, and here that means “meaning.” Even understood in this way, Being is in being—the “most in being”—but in the sense of supreme finitude.
