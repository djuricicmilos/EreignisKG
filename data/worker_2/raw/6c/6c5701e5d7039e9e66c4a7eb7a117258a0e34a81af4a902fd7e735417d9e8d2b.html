II. The Central Question of Western Metaphysics


12. Unfolding the Guide


The “from within” and “from without” — time-space. How in relation to “time” and “space”? “Time from within” and “from without.” (Ecstatic) rapturous collision—the succession of nows. That it is itself the very basis of this distinction, which, posited in such spatial terms, has not yet been sufficiently grasped. How the question of time-space—and thus that of being-there—lies within this inside-outside!



13. The Structure of the Central Question


1. Beingness from the perspective of the being

2. Being from itself, i.e., the truth of its essence.

3. Because 1. returns to 2. through the fundamental question.


The Field: Being as a whole (that which is being questioned).

The Goal: Being as such (beingness)—that which is being questioned (the being of the being, not merely “being”).

The perspective: The being of the existing, i.e., the existing in terms of its constitution and mode of being.

The criterion (by what and how it is measured and given): All of Being, where this reveals itself most fully and most highly in the light of the undisclosed concept of Being. E.g., for Descartes, indubitatum and certum; or even ὁυσία!? The locus of the familiarity and authority of Being.

The location of questioning: (“In the midst of”)

↓Opposite – ἀντί
Against – Presence

The questioner himself: Man—ζῶον λόγον ἔχον.

The response of the questioned: Man belonging to Being. As corresponding to Being as such, a measure. Inclusion in the questioned—Being as such. The turn, in everything, in various ways.
