25. A Philosophical Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics

Research that used to be carried out on an ad hoc basis is becoming less certain; but that only means that the safeguards are becoming more comprehensive. Security is becoming more all-encompassing.

The question of who “we” are—for whom the “object” is ever an object— can no longer be avoided. But what does this mean? Anthropological dissection? In this way, even human beings were turned into the “object” of yet another fleeting subject. It is crucial to examine the subject-object-relationship as a relationship in its essence and fundamental nature, and to remove this inquiry from the realm of “epistemology” and “the metaphysics of knowledge.”
