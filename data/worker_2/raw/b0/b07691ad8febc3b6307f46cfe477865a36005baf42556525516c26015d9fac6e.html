II. Die Leitfrage der abendländischen Metaphysik


7. On the Characterization of the Guiding Question


What is Being? Being is 1. taken as the Real (that which is fully present and available); not in terms of possibility and necessity, or even more fundamentally. Possibility and necessity are understood solely in terms of reality. (Cf. Aristotle, Met. Θ 8, 1049b: The priority of ἐνέργεια over δύναμις can only be understood in the Greek sense! ἔργον, φύσις (τέχνη) πρότερον ἐνέργεια δύναμεώς ἐστιν). The real as that which is present, and indeed constantly present. This presence is again interpreted in various ways: φύσις – ἰδέα – certum ens – object – that which is absolutely known; that which transcends the present, elevating our “life”: Nietzsche.

2. Being, thus conceived—stands, with regard to its interpretation, in relation to νοεῖν—λόγος—statement—ratio—reason—spirit, and indeed to cognition and thought in general. “Being and Thinking”—the title for the fundamental movement of Western metaphysics within the central question.



8. The Systematics (the Structure) of the Guiding Question (τί τὸ ὅν)


This refers to the structure, composition, and interrelationship of what is “at issue” in this question and its posing.

The structure:What is being asked?The Being of what is.
What is being asked about?Concerning being as a whole.
From where is the question being asked?
Who is asking the question?
What is his relationship to the interviewer and the interviewee?
(Being as a whole.)

Bringing the structure into one’s awareness in order to submit to it fully and clearly—that is, to truly ask. And thus unfold the guiding question and that is to say, now first from the experience of the guide, as opposed to direct treatment.
