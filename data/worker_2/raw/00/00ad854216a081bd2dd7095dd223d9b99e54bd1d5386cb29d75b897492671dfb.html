III. Correctness—Certainty

23. The World and Objectivity


The objective binds behavior to representation as a presentation of the appended. This “presentation” is an assignment to places and, prior to that, the organizing determination of a multiplicity of places (workstation—“place” and location; “place” is even more closely tied to the structure than “location”). The concrete compels behavior into the self-alignment of a marching order. The concrete takes away the possibility of coexistence in the sense of the originally “existent” being-in-the-world. The unconditional form of the assigning order is devastation in the guise of boundless consumption, which has caused everything original to vanish. The historical solidarity of determination is shaken by power as the essence of objectivity. In its place come organization and mobilization.

The measures are adjustments and abrasions aimed at establishing oneself within unconditional predictability. Unanimity with this now characterizes conformity (i.e., correctness).

Measures are precautions of appropriation. Appropriation admits only the concrete, in order to make it disappear once again into the expanded and more secure realm of the concrete. In the measures of appropriation, a de-worlding of the world prevails. Human beings are attuned to the concrete as the “world,” which is a non-world.

In modern times, reification is a refusal to let the world be, without, however, being able to detach oneself from the world.



24. Quantum Mechanics and Reification


Quantum mechanics and reification experience, in their own approach, the inevitability of the reifying process itself and thus the relationship of reification objectification [Verge-genständlichung] (Location, Orientation, View, Path, and Design) to include in the statement about the object.
